The Washek Program Sign up

Summer camp for 5th graders.

Sports, games, and arts. Small group — 20 kids max. About $10 an hour. Run by John and Luke Washek.

Who we are

John is a University of Michigan engineering student who has been coaching basketball for kids this age for years. Luke co-runs the camp day-to-day. We grew up around a lot of the kids already signed up — this isn't a business, it's a camp we wanted to exist.

We keep it small on purpose. Every kid gets real attention, nobody sits out, and parents can always check in.

What to expect

  • Different activity each day — basketball, football, arts, games
  • Teams compete across the week, then reset every Friday with new teams
  • Touch football only — no tackling, strict rule
  • Max 20 kids, always supervised
  • ~$10/hr, paid in person — no apps, no subscriptions
  • You pick which days work. No pressure to come every day.
